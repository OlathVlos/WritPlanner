# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-26
- First release for WoW Forever (Interface 16001).
- Plan your run: cheapest gold per rep within your budget, rep target (dropdown: next standing, 250–5000, or no target) and level. Faction, rep and prices follow your character automatically.
- Reminder that each named writ can only be handed in once a day (tooltips, Plan your run, Road to Exalted).
- Plan your run headings sort on click: tick columns (not ticked / ticked first), Name and Zone and NPC A–Z / Z–A, Rep and Cost low–high / high–low; third click returns to planned order.
- Auto-ticking checklist (writ in bags, items in bags, turned in today) with manual ticks and Clear ticks; resets at the daily reset. Danger level (Deadly/Risky/Even/Easy/Trivial) has its own column. Tooltips show how many are listed on the AH.
- Road to Exalted: a day-by-day plan from your current rep to Exalted at current prices, with an optional daily budget, the standings reached each day, and one click to load today's writs. Click any day for its items, item and writ prices (live), cost and rep, and send that day's shopping list to Auctionator. Each named writ is handed in at most once a day; spare copies and items in your bags are used up on later days before buying more.
- Merchant's Favor: every Waylaid Crate ranked by gold per Favor with its cheapest fill at live prices (hover for all options), Favor balance, reward goals, crates-in-bags-first planning with an optional buy-crates-on-AH mode, and an Auctionator list.
- Run complete banner, Clear run, Plan another run today.
- Find writs: Writ, Item, Turn-in NPC, Tier, Rep, Continent and Zone filters (one search at a time, like the website), Clear filters and Reset all. Click a writ to add it to or remove it from your run. Click a column heading to sort (Writ A–Z / Z–A; Zone A–Z / Z–A; Rep, Level, Cost and /100 rep low–high / high–low).
- Shopping list, with one-click export to an Auctionator shopping list.
- Prices from GoldScout, Auctionator or TradeSkillMaster when installed. No prices are bundled; without a price addon it runs in rep-only mode.
- Licensed GPL-3.0-or-later (turn-in data derived from QuestieDB; see NOTICE.md).
- TomTom waypoints (or a Blizzard map pin without TomTom).
- Optional auction house scan for players without a price addon (read-only, throttled, only on button press; about 25 seconds for the whole auction house), saved per realm and faction; a Writ Planner button at the end of the auction house tab row (left-click open, right-click scan).
- Standalone window: opens anywhere and doesn't close other windows. Minimap button, Addon Compartment entry, /writplan.
